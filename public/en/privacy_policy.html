Privacy Policy

This English version is provided for reference only. If there is any discrepancy between this translation and the Korean original, the Korean version prevails.

SSOBIG (hereinafter the "Company") hereby establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy as follows, pursuant to Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act, in order to protect the personal information of data subjects and to handle related grievances promptly and smoothly.

Article 1 (Purposes of Processing Personal Information)

The Company processes personal information for the following purposes. Personal information being processed will not be used for any purpose other than the following, and if the purpose of use changes, the Company will take necessary measures, such as obtaining separate consent pursuant to Article 18 of the Personal Information Protection Act.

1. Membership Registration and Management

  • Confirming the intention to register as a member
  • Identification and authentication of the individual in connection with the provision of membership-based services
  • Maintenance and management of membership status
  • Identity verification pursuant to the limited identity verification system
  • Prevention of fraudulent use of the service
  • Confirming whether the consent of a legal representative has been obtained when processing personal information of children under the age of 14
  • Various notices and notifications
  • Handling of grievances

2. Provision of Gathering/Event Services

  • Creating and participating in gatherings
  • Providing real-time voting and survey services
  • Providing participant matching services
  • Providing QR code-based interaction services
  • Providing chat and direct message services
  • Providing customized content
  • Identity verification
  • Age verification

3. Marketing Information and Provision of Benefits

  • Providing advertising information, such as events, benefits, coupons and new content, to users who have optionally consented
  • Sending marketing information via text message, KakaoTalk, email, etc.
  • Managing whether consent to receive marketing has been given and the history of withdrawals
  • Granting coupon or token benefits in accordance with consent to receive marketing, and preventing duplicate grants

Article 2 (Processing and Retention Period of Personal Information)

The Company processes and retains personal information within the retention and use period of personal information prescribed by laws, or within the retention and use period of personal information consented to by the data subject at the time of collection.

1. Principles of Processing and Retention

In the following cases, the Company does not use or provide personal information until the relevant reason ceases to exist.

  • Where an investigation, inquiry, etc. due to a violation of relevant laws is in progress
  • Where claims and obligations arising from the use of the service remain outstanding

2. Processing and Retention Period of Personal Information

1) Membership registration and management: Until withdrawal of membership - provided, however, that in the following cases, until the relevant reason ceases to exist

* Where an investigation, inquiry, etc. due to a violation of relevant laws is in progress

* Where claims and obligations arising from the use of the service remain outstanding

2) Records related to gathering/event services: Up to 3 months after the gathering ends

3) Records related to marketing information and provision of benefits: Until consent to receive is withdrawn or membership is withdrawn. However, records necessary for preventing duplicate grants of coupon or token benefits, evidencing consent history, and responding to disputes may be retained for the necessary period in accordance with relevant laws and internal retention standards.

Article 3 (Entrustment of Personal Information Processing)

For the smooth handling of personal information, the Company entrusts personal information processing tasks as follows.

- Trustee: Kakao Corp. (카카오(주))

- Entrusted tasks: Sending KakaoTalk-based AlimTalk notifications and messages

- Trustee: Text message and email delivery service providers

- Entrusted tasks: Sending notices and advertising information via text message, email, etc.

2. Management of Entrustment Contracts

When concluding an entrustment contract, the Company documents and manages the following matters pursuant to Article 25 of the Personal Information Protection Act.

  • Prohibition of processing personal information for purposes other than performing the entrusted tasks
  • Technical and managerial protective measures
  • Restrictions on re-entrustment
  • Management and supervision of the trustee
  • Matters concerning liability, such as compensation for damages

3. Notice of Changes to Entrusted Tasks

If the details of the entrusted tasks or the trustee change, the Company will disclose such changes without delay through this Privacy Policy.

Article 4 (Rights and Obligations of Data Subjects and Legal Representatives, and Methods of Exercise)

Data subjects may exercise the following rights related to personal information protection against the Company at any time.

  • Request to access personal information
  • Request for correction in case of errors, etc.
  • Request for deletion
  • Request for suspension of processing

The rights under Paragraph 1 may be exercised by the following methods.

  • In writing
  • By telephone
  • By email
  • By facsimile (FAX)

Where a data subject requests correction or deletion of errors, etc. in personal information, the Company will not use or provide the relevant personal information until the correction or deletion is completed.

Rights may be exercised through an agent, such as the data subject's legal representative or a person delegated by the data subject. In this case, a power of attorney in accordance with Annexed Form No. 11 of the Enforcement Rule of the Personal Information Protection Act must be submitted.

Data subjects must not, in violation of relevant laws, infringe upon the personal information or privacy of themselves or others being processed by the Company.

Article 5 (Consent to Receive Marketing Information and Withdrawal)

Only where a user has optionally consented, the Company may provide advertising information, such as events, benefits, coupons and new content, via text message, KakaoTalk, email, etc.

Consent to receive marketing information is optional, and there is no restriction on the use of the service even if you do not consent. Users may withdraw their consent to receive at any time through My Page in the app or through the contact details of the personal information protection officer.

Coupon or token benefits granted in accordance with consent to receive marketing are granted only once, the first time, per campaign, and even if a user withdraws consent and then consents again, the same benefit will not be granted again.

Article 6 (Items of Personal Information Processed)

Required items for membership registration and management:

  • User ID
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Real name
  • Gender
  • Date of birth

Required items of information related to service use:

  • Gathering participation records
  • Voting/survey response data
  • Matching records
  • QR code scan records
  • Chat/direct message content

Items related to marketing information and provision of benefits:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • User ID
  • Whether consent to receive marketing has been given
  • Date and time of consent to receive marketing or withdrawal
  • Channel of consent to receive marketing or withdrawal
  • Wording of the consent to receive marketing and its version
  • Whether benefits for consent to receive marketing have been granted, and grant history

Automatically generated information: information automatically generated and collected in the course of using the service:

  • IP address
  • Access logs
  • Service usage records
  • Records of improper use

Article 7 (Destruction of Personal Information)

When personal information becomes unnecessary, such as upon the expiry of the retention period or the achievement of the purpose of processing, the Company destroys the relevant personal information without delay.

Exceptional retention: Where personal information must continue to be retained pursuant to other laws even though the retention period of personal information consented to by the data subject has expired or the purpose of processing has been achieved, the relevant personal information is retained by transferring it to a separate database (DB) or storing it in a different location.

Procedure and method of destruction 1) Destruction procedure - Selection of personal information for which a reason for destruction has arisen - Approval by the Chief Privacy Officer - Execution of the destruction of personal information

2) Destruction method - Electronic files: Destroyed so that the records cannot be reproduced - Paper documents: Shredded with a shredder or incinerated

Article 8 (Measures to Ensure the Security of Personal Information)

The Company takes the following measures to ensure the security of personal information.

  • Managerial measures - Establishment and operation of an information protection management system - Minimization of personal information handlers and regular training - Establishment of an internal management plan and periodic review/update
  • Technical measures - Encrypted storage and management of personal information - Restriction of access rights and operation of an access control system - Retention of access records for personal information processing systems and prevention of forgery/alteration - Use of the HTTPS security protocol - Installation and operation of security programs such as antivirus software
  • Physical measures - Access control for cloud servers - Management of access rights to personal information storage systems

Article 9 (Installation, Operation and Refusal of Devices that Automatically Collect Personal Information)

The Company uses "cookies," which store usage information and retrieve it from time to time, in order to provide users with individually customized services.

Cookies are small pieces of information sent by the server (http) used to operate the website to the user's computer browser, and may also be stored on the hard disk of the user's PC.

Details Regarding Cookies

1) Purposes of using cookies - Maintaining login status - Maintaining users' gathering participation information and settings - Understanding service usage patterns - Providing information optimized for users

2) Installation, operation and refusal of cookies: Users may refuse the storage of cookies by setting options in their web browser.

- Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data

- Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data

3) Restrictions upon refusal of cookies: If you refuse the storage of cookies, you may experience difficulty using services that require login.
