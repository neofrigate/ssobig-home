Refund Policy

This English version is provided for reference only. If there is any discrepancy between this translation and the Korean original, the Korean version prevails.

1. Refund Request Period

A refund may be requested for any payment within 7 days from the date of payment.
However, no refund will be given if any portion of the tokens has been used, or if any Pro Membership benefit has been used, within that period.

2. Nature of Digital Products

The tokens and memberships of this Service are intangible digital products that can be used immediately upon payment.
Since a full refund may be difficult once use has commenced after payment, please make your decision carefully before purchasing.

3. Token Refund Rules

  • Full refund of unused tokens: A full refund is available if there is no record of token use whatsoever within 7 days from the date of payment.
  • Refund after partial use: No refund will be given after partial use.

4. Pro Membership Refund Rules

  • No record of use whatsoever within 7 days: A full refund is available if no benefit of any kind has been used within 7 days from the date of membership payment.
  • Refund after partial use: No refund will be given if 1 day has passed since the membership start date, or if membership benefits have been used after the membership was purchased.
  • Payments with a promotion/discount applied: Upon a refund, the refund amount may be recalculated based on the regular price.

5. Refund Procedure

Where there is no record of use whatsoever (within 7 days)

You may apply for a refund yourself via the menu Payment History > Select the relevant payment item > Apply for Refund.

Where a refund is requested for other understandable reasons

Submit a refund request via My Page > Customer Center.

6. Non-Refundable Items

  • Tokens provided free of charge and additional benefits obtained through promotions
  • Other benefits designated by the Company as non-refundable (where notice has been given in advance)

7. Inquiries and Customer Center

For inquiries regarding refunds, please contact the Customer Center below.
Customer Center phone number: 02-2645-7942

8. Agreement to Terms

By using and making payment for this Service, you are deemed to have agreed to the above terms and refund policy.

9. Notes

  • The usage period and the refundable period of charged tokens are limited to within 1 year from the time of payment.
  • Charged tokens may not be transferred between users.
