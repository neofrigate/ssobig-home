Refund Policy

1. Refund request period

For all payments, you may request a refund within 7 days of the payment date.
However, no refund is available if you have used any tokens or Pro membership benefits during that period.

2. Nature of digital products

The tokens and memberships offered by this service are intangible digital products available for use immediately after payment.
Once use has begun after payment, a full refund may be difficult. Please consider your purchase carefully.

3. Token refunds

  • Full refund for unused tokens: A full refund is available within 7 days of payment if there is no record of any token use.
  • Refund after partial use: No refund is available after partial use.

4. Pro membership refunds

  • No use within 7 days: A full refund is available if no benefits have been used within 7 days of the membership payment date.
  • Refund after partial use: No refund is available if 1 day has passed since the membership start date or if any membership benefits have been used after purchase.
  • Promotional or discounted payments: The refund amount may be recalculated using the regular price.

5. Refund procedure

No use at all (within 7 days)

You can request a refund directly via Payment history > Select the payment item > Request a refund.

Refund requests for other understandable reasons

Submit your refund request via My Page > Customer Support.

6. Non-refundable items

  • Tokens granted free of charge and additional promotional benefits
  • Other benefits designated by the Company as non-refundable, where disclosed in advance

7. Inquiries and customer support

For refund inquiries, please contact Customer Support below.

8. Agreement to the terms

By using this service and making a payment, you are deemed to have agreed to the above terms and refund policy.

9. Important notes

  • The use period and refund eligibility period for purchased tokens are limited to within 1 year of payment.
  • Purchased tokens cannot be transferred between users.
