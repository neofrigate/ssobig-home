Refund Policy

1. Refund request period

Tokens and Pro passes are eligible for a full refund of the amount actually paid within 7 days of payment if the purchased product has not been used.
We do not offer prorated refunds for ordinary early termination. However, refunds required due to the Company’s fault or applicable law will be provided under the applicable rules, regardless of this policy’s time limits, usage conditions or restrictions on prorated refunds.

2. Nature of digital products

Tokens and Pro passes are digital products available immediately after payment. Pro is a one-time pass valid for 1 month from payment, with no automatic renewal or recurring charges.
To continue using Pro after the pass expires, a separate purchase is required. Please review the product, validity period and refund terms before purchasing.

3. Token refunds

  • Unused tokens: Within 7 days of payment, the amount actually paid is fully refundable if none of the tokens from that purchase have been used.
  • Ordinary refunds after partial token use are not offered. The exceptions in section 1 apply to refunds due to the Company’s fault or required by law.

4. Pro one-time pass refunds

  • A Pro pass is eligible for a full refund of the amount actually paid within 7 days of payment if none of that pass’s benefits have been used.
  • An unused-pass refund is not restricted merely because 1 day has passed since the start date. Ordinary early termination does not qualify for a prorated refund; the exceptions in section 1 apply to Company fault and statutory refunds.
  • Promotional or discounted purchases also receive a full refund of the amount actually paid when the full-refund conditions above are met.

5. Refund procedure

No use at all (within 7 days)

You can request a refund directly via Payment history > Select the payment item > Request a refund.

Company fault, statutory refund grounds, or difficulty requesting a refund directly

Submit your refund request via My Page > Customer Support.

6. Non-refundable items

  • Tokens granted free of charge and additional promotional benefits
  • Free benefits cannot be exchanged for a cash refund. This does not restrict refund rights for paid purchases or refunds due to Company fault or required by law.

8. Agreement to the terms

By using this service and making a payment, you are deemed to have agreed to the above terms and refund policy.

9. Important notes

  • Purchased tokens have a validity period of 1 year from payment. Refunds follow the criteria above without limiting rights under applicable law.
  • Purchased tokens cannot be transferred between users.
