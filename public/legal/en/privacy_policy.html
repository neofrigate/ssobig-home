Privacy Policy

SsoBig (the “Company”) establishes and publishes this Privacy Policy under Article 30 of Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act to protect data subjects’ personal information and promptly and smoothly handle related complaints.

Article 1 (Purposes of Processing Personal Information)

The Company processes personal information for the following purposes only. If the purposes change, it will take necessary measures, such as obtaining separate consent, under Article 18 of Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act.

1. Membership registration and management

  • Confirming the intention to register
  • Identifying and authenticating users for membership services
  • Maintaining and managing membership status
  • Identity verification under the limited identity verification system
  • Preventing misuse of the Service
  • Verifying legal representative consent when processing personal information of children under 14
  • Sending various notices and notifications
  • Handling complaints

2. Providing gathering/event services

  • Creating and participating in gatherings
  • Providing real-time voting and survey services
  • Providing participant matching services
  • Providing QR code-based interaction services
  • Providing chat and private messaging services
  • Providing personalized content
  • Identity verification
  • Age verification

3. Marketing communications and benefits

  • Sending advertising information about events, benefits, coupons and new content to users who have optionally consented
  • Sending marketing information by SMS, KakaoTalk, email and other means
  • Managing marketing consent status and withdrawal history
  • Granting coupon or token benefits for marketing consent and preventing duplicate grants

Article 2 (Processing and Retention Periods)

The Company processes and retains personal information within the retention and use periods prescribed by law or consented to by the data subject when the information was collected.

1. Processing and retention principles

In the following cases, the Company does not use or provide personal information until the relevant circumstances end.

  • An investigation or inquiry into a violation of applicable laws is ongoing
  • Claims or debts arising from service use remain outstanding

2. Personal information processing and retention periods

1) Membership registration and management: Until withdrawal from membership, except in the following cases, when retention continues until the circumstances end

* An investigation or inquiry into a violation of applicable laws is ongoing

* Claims or debts arising from service use remain outstanding

2) Gathering/event service records: Until 3 months after the gathering ends

3) Marketing communications and benefits records: Until withdrawal of consent or membership. Records needed to prevent duplicate coupon or token benefits, prove consent history or address disputes may be retained for the necessary period under applicable laws and internal retention standards.

Article 3 (Outsourcing of Personal Information Processing)

The Company outsources personal information processing as follows to facilitate its operations.

- Processor: Kakao Corp.

- Outsourced work: Sending KakaoTalk Alimtalk and messages

- Processors: SMS and email delivery service providers

- Outsourced work: Sending notices and advertising information by SMS, email and other means

2. Management of outsourcing agreements

When entering into outsourcing agreements, the Company documents and manages the following matters under Article 25 of Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act.

  • Prohibition on processing personal information for purposes other than the outsourced work
  • Technical and administrative safeguards
  • Restrictions on subcontracting
  • Management and supervision of processors
  • Liability, including compensation for damage

3. Notice of changes to outsourced work

Changes to the outsourced work or processors will be disclosed without delay through this Privacy Policy.

Article 4 (Rights and Obligations of Data Subjects and Legal Representatives, and How to Exercise Them)

Data subjects may exercise the following personal information protection rights against the Company at any time.

  • Request access to personal information
  • Request correction of errors
  • Request deletion
  • Request suspension of processing

The rights in paragraph 1 may be exercised through:

  • Written request
  • Telephone
  • Email
  • Fax

If a data subject requests correction or deletion of personal information, the Company does not use or provide that information until the correction or deletion is complete.

Rights may be exercised through a legal representative or another authorized agent. In that case, a power of attorney in the form prescribed in Annex Form No. 11 of the Enforcement Rule of Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act must be submitted.

Data subjects must not violate applicable laws by infringing their own or another person’s personal information or privacy processed by the Company.

Article 5 (Marketing Consent and Withdrawal)

Only when users optionally consent may the Company send advertising information about events, benefits, coupons and new content by SMS, KakaoTalk, email or other means.

Marketing consent is optional, and refusing it does not restrict service use. Users may withdraw consent at any time through My Page in the app or by contacting the person responsible for personal information protection.

Coupon or token benefits for marketing consent are granted only once per campaign. Withdrawing and later giving consent again does not result in the same benefit being granted again.

Article 6 (Personal Information Processed)

Required information for membership registration and management:

  • User ID
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Real name
  • Gender
  • Date of birth

Required service usage information:

  • Gathering participation records
  • Voting/survey response data
  • Matching records
  • QR code scan records
  • Chat/private message content

Information related to marketing communications and benefits:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • User ID
  • Marketing consent status
  • Date and time of marketing consent or withdrawal
  • Channel used to give or withdraw marketing consent
  • Marketing consent wording and its version
  • Status and history of benefits granted for marketing consent

Automatically generated information collected during service use:

  • IP address
  • Access logs
  • Service usage records
  • Misuse records

Article 7 (Destruction of Personal Information)

The Company destroys personal information without delay when it is no longer needed, such as when the retention period expires or its processing purpose has been achieved.

Exceptional retention: If another law requires continued retention after the consented retention period expires or the processing purpose has been achieved, the information is moved to a separate database or stored in a separate location.

Destruction procedures and methods — 1) Procedure: Select information for which a destruction reason has arisen; obtain approval from the privacy officer; destroy the information.

2) Methods: Electronic files are destroyed so records cannot be recovered. Paper documents are shredded or incinerated.

Article 8 (Security Measures for Personal Information)

The Company takes the following measures to ensure the security of personal information.

  • Administrative measures: Establishing and operating an information security management system; minimizing personnel handling personal information and training them regularly; establishing and periodically reviewing/updating internal management plans.
  • Technical measures: Encrypted storage and management; restricting access privileges and operating access controls; retaining access logs for processing systems and preventing forgery or alteration; using HTTPS; installing and operating antivirus and other security software.
  • Physical measures: Controlling access to cloud servers and managing access privileges for personal information storage systems.

Article 9 (Installation, Operation and Rejection of Automatic Collection Technologies)

The Company uses cookies, which store and retrieve usage information, to provide personalized services.

Cookies are small pieces of information sent by the server (HTTP) operating a website to a user’s computer browser and may be stored on the computer’s hard disk.

Cookie details

1) Purposes: Maintaining login status; retaining gathering participation information and settings; understanding service usage patterns; providing optimized information.

2) Installation, operation and rejection: Users may reject cookie storage through their web browser settings.

- Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data

- Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies and website data

3) Restrictions when rejecting cookies: Rejecting cookie storage may make services requiring login difficult to use.
